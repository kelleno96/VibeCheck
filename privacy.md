# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 3, 2026

VibeCheck does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app stores

VibeCheck saves your daily mood entries (a color selection and date) and your preferences (notification time and color theme) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

## No analytics or tracking

VibeCheck does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. There are no third-party SDKs that collect data.

## No network access

VibeCheck does not connect to the internet. All data is stored on-device only.

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach me at [kodevsupport@icloud.com](kodevsupport@icloud.com).
