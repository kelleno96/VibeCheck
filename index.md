# VibeCheck

VibeCheck is a simple mood tracking app that helps you log how you're feeling each day, spot patterns over time, and keep your mind a little quieter.

## Features

- **Daily mood logging** — quickly record your mood with a tap
- **Yearly mosaic** — see your whole year of moods at a glance
- **Home screen widget** — check in without even opening the app
- **Color themes** — personalise the look to match your vibe
